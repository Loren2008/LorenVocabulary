# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in LorenVocabulary a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials or personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading technical claims.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or temporarily or permanently ban contributions and participants when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Enforcement decisions should be explained when practical and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.
